# Casset Vision v1.0

## Product goal
Build a professional, extensible Iranian audio creator platform that attracts podcasters, singers and other creators, lets them publish and share their work, and gives them trustworthy playback statistics and a future path to rewards/monetization.

## MVP goal
The first release is not the complete future platform. It is the smallest professional product that can acquire real users and validate the core loop:

Creator joins → publishes audio → listener plays → platform validates the play → statistics update → reward/points update → creator sees results.

## Product principles
- Trustworthy plays over inflated vanity metrics.
- Creator experience must be simple enough for repeated publishing.
- Public profiles and statistics must feel professional.
- The architecture must support future scale without premature distributed-system complexity.
- Real usage data will guide future recommendation, monetization and anti-fraud sophistication.
