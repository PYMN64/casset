# Casset Product Definition v1.0

## Primary actors
- Listener: discovers and plays content.
- Creator: publishes content and monitors performance.
- Moderator/Admin: protects content quality and platform integrity.

## MVP capabilities
- account registration and verification
- creator onboarding and profile
- audio upload and validation
- track/album lifecycle
- moderation workflow
- playback and qualified-play detection
- basic anti-fraud signals
- point/reward ledger
- creator analytics dashboard
- search/explore/trending foundation
- administration and audit controls

## Deferred capabilities
Advanced recommendation, AI recommendation, complex social systems, chat, marketplace/payout complexity and distributed infrastructure are outside the first release gate.
